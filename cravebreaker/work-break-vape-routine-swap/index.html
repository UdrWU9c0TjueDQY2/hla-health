Replace the job of the break.

      
A vape break may have been more than nicotine: it could mark a pause, change the view, occupy the hands or create social time. A realistic swap keeps the useful part of the pause while changing the routine linked to vaping.

      

        
1
Change the first move.
Plan where your feet, hands or attention go when the break starts.

        
2
Match the time you have.
A two-minute break needs a different plan from a fifteen-minute lunch pause.

        
3
Keep a backup.
If the first activity is not enough, change location or reach for qualified support instead of treating the plan as a test of willpower.

      

      

        
Why plan the work break?

        
Health Canada lists routines and work situations among vaping triggers and gives a short walk with a colleague as one example of an alternative. Smokefree.gov specifically names work breaks and recommends replacement behaviours, changing patterns and planning for downtime. Sources checked November 4, 2026.

      

    


    

      
What this card cannot do.

      

        
It cannot stop withdrawal.
Nicotine withdrawal can be difficult and may need professional support.

        
It cannot guarantee a craving ends.
The card is a plan for the routine, not a promise about what your body will do.

        
It cannot replace workplace rules.
Use only safe, permitted activities and locations for your job.

      

      
If you are struggling with nicotine withdrawal, talk with a doctor, pharmacist or quitline about appropriate support. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

What can I do instead of vaping on a work break?
Keep the break but plan a different first minute, place and activity. Official quit guidance suggests changing routines, using replacement behaviours and planning for downtime. The best swap depends on what the break was doing for you and what your workplace allows.

        
Will this planner stop a nicotine craving?
No. It organizes a routine swap and cannot guarantee that a craving will stop. Nicotine withdrawal can need professional support; a doctor, pharmacist or quitline can discuss appropriate options.

        
Does this page save my choices?
No. The plan is built in the current browser tab and is not transmitted or stored by this page. Refreshing or closing the page clears it.

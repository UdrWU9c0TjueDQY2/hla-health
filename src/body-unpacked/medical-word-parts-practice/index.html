USE IT IN REAL LIFE

Use a word part as a starting clue.

Cover the meaning column and say the connection aloud. Then cover the word parts and work backward. If one pair stays difficult, put it beside a different pair and explain the difference in your own words.

Eight body word parts to keep nearby
Word partBody meaning
cardio-heart
hepato-liver
nephro-kidney
osteo-bone
dermato-skin
gastro-stomach
myo-muscle
cyto-cell

Spelling can change when parts join. These eight pairs are a small learning set, not a complete dictionary. Recognizing a root does not tell you whether a person has a condition, how serious it is or what to do about it.

Try this next: pick one body meaning—such as bone—and look for its connections to nutrients and physiological roles in Body Unpacked. That turns an isolated label into a connected idea.

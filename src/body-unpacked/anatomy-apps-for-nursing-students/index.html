First-year nursing has two different questions wearing one name. "Where is the brachial plexus" is anatomy, and you want a 3D atlas for it. "What happens to the iron in that spinach, and why does B12 need the stomach" is physiology, and an atlas will shrug. Most app lists treat them as one purchase. This one does not.

Atlas-first

Buy the biggest 3D model on day one. Fine if the school is paying. Expensive if it is you, and silent on physiology.

Free everything

Flashcards, a free skeleton app, lecture slides. Gets you through a practical. Thin when the question is "why".

Match the module

One atlas (paid once or free), one memorising tool, and one sourced physiology app. That is the list below, and ours is the last of the three, not the first.

Trace the route of inhaled air

Start just below the voice box and follow four landmarks to the air sacs. Choose one structure at a time. Each first-try answer earns one point.

Read the route and explanations
  1. The trachea is the windpipe below the voice box.
  2. The trachea branches into the main bronchi, one going toward each lung.
  3. Within the lungs, the bronchial branches lead to narrower bronchioles.
  4. The route ends at alveoli, small air sacs where oxygen and carbon dioxide exchange with the blood.

Source: National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute: The Respiratory System. This exercise covers four landmarks, rather than every airway subdivision.

The six, from their listings

"Paying" lines quote each App Store listing on 4 November 2026; prices and tiers change. We quote no ratings or download counts for anyone.

1. Complete Anatomy

3D4Medical (Elsevier) · listing, checked 4 November 2026

Paying: Free to download with a 3-day trial; subscriptions charged annually and auto-renewing, per the listing.

Honestly: The full-body 3D model most lecturers mean when they say "the anatomy app". It is the deepest atlas here and it is a subscription you will be paying for in year three.

Pick it for: Where structures are, in 3D, for the whole programme.

2. Human Anatomy Atlas 2027

Visible Body · listing, checked 4 November 2026

Paying: A one-time purchase (24.99 at the time of checking) with optional in-app purchases for physiology animations and dental content.

Honestly: The one-time-price answer to Complete Anatomy. Strong 3D atlas, lab activities included; the physiology side is an add-on.

Pick it for: A 3D atlas you pay for once.

3. TeachMe Anatomy

TeachMeSeries · listing, checked 4 November 2026

Paying: Free with an optional Pro membership by in-app subscription (monthly, quarterly or annual), ad-free with 3D models and quizzes.

Honestly: Text-first and clinically flavoured, which suits nursing better than some atlases. The free tier is readable; Pro removes ads and adds the models.

Pick it for: Reading the anatomy, with clinical relevance, on a budget.

4. 3D Bones and Muscles

YingLiang Ma · listing, checked 4 November 2026

Paying: Completely free; the listing says no in-app purchase.

Honestly: Musculoskeletal only, and it looks it. For bones and muscles with no money at all it is hard to argue with.

Pick it for: The skeleton and muscles, free, nothing else.

5. Daily Anatomy Flashcards

Kenhub · listing, checked 4 November 2026

Paying: Free to download; the listing text we checked describes 500 structures in a flashcard format and does not state pricing, so check the in-app terms.

Honestly: Not an atlas: spaced-repetition flashcards for the 500 structures that come up most. The right tool for the week before a practical.

Pick it for: Memorising names before an exam.

6. Body Unpacked (ours)

Honest Little Apps · listing, checked 4 November 2026

Paying: Free to download; no account, no ads, no analytics, everything offline, per the listing.

Honestly: Not an anatomy atlas, and we would be lying to put it in that column. It is physiology and nutrition: follow a nutrient after you swallow it on a body map, step through digestion and circulation, see which claims have which kind of evidence. Illustrations are simplified and AI-assisted, and the content is checked by the developer against public sources, not independently reviewed by a clinician.

Pick it for: The "what does it do and where" half of A&P, with sources, after the atlas has told you where things are.

Amara has a bone practical on Thursday and an A&P exam the week after. She bought the big atlas in week one because everyone did. Questions students ask

What is the best anatomy app for nursing students?

There is no single one, which is why this page has six. For the atlas, Complete Anatomy if a subscription is fine and Human Anatomy Atlas if you would rather pay once. For reading with clinical context, TeachMe Anatomy. For the week before a practical, Kenhub's flashcards. For physiology and nutrition with sources, ours. All of that is from the listings on 4 November 2026; they change.

Do I need to pay for an anatomy app?

Not in first term. 3D Bones and Muscles is free with no in-app purchase, TeachMe Anatomy's free tier is readable, and Body Unpacked is free. Pay when a module needs a full-body 3D model and your library's access does not cover it; many nursing schools license Complete Anatomy or Visible Body, so ask before buying.

Is Body Unpacked an anatomy app?

No. It covers physiology and nutrition: nutrient journeys on a body map, digestion, breathing and circulation step-throughs, the reflex arc, an evidence lens on popular health claims, and a 249-topic sourced atlas with Simple, Standard and Deep dive levels. If your assignment is "label the brachial plexus", it is the wrong app and one of the five above is right.

Why is your own app in the list?

Because it answers half the question nursing students ask, and leaving it out would be odd. It is last, labelled, and the honest line is that it complements an atlas rather than replacing one.

Is the content clinically reviewed?

Body Unpacked's listing says content is checked by the developer against the cited public sources and has not been independently reviewed by a clinician, and that it provides general educational information only. We would rather quote that than soften it. For the other apps, read their listings; we do not characterise their review processes.

Can I use it offline on placement?

Body Unpacked bundles all learning content and works without a connection; the listing says no account, no ads, no cloud AI. The other apps vary; Complete Anatomy and Visible Body download large models, and TeachMe Anatomy is partly web content.

Bedtime transition lab

Can both evening bathroom turns fit?

Compare two planned turns in one shared bathroom. If they overlap, find the earliest time the second person can start.

Keep the first turn fixed. The second turn keeps its preferred time if both fit. When they overlap, the second turn moves after the first.

Example below

A ten-minute overlap

If the first turn runs from 9:30 to 9:50 p.m. and the second was planned for 9:40 to 9:50 p.m., both people need the room for the same ten minutes. Keeping the first turn fixed moves the second to 9:50–10:00 p.m.

Touching endpoints fit: one person can start when the other finishes. A second turn entirely before the first also fits. Times are local clock times on the dates selected; the planner does not adjust for clock changes.

Use the map honestly

Three rules for a useful constraint map.

The goal is not to fill every box. It is to prevent a routine from being built on time, space or equipment that does not exist.

1

Map the smallest real window

If five minutes is the reliable window, choose five. The tool reports your choice without upgrading it.

2

Name access, not intention

Select equipment you can actually reach this week. Owning equipment and having it available are different facts.

3

Carry constraints forward

Use the summary when choosing or discussing a routine. A plan that ignores the map has not solved the scheduling problem.

What it cannot do

Decide whether exercise is safe for you.

This page does not assess symptoms, medication, injury, readiness, nutrition needs or exercise suitability. It does not prescribe movements, duration, intensity or frequency. Follow your clinician's instructions. If you have a medical condition, recent injury or fracture, dizziness or pain, check with your clinician before exercising.

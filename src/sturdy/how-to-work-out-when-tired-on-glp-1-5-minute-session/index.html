The fix

Today

Sore day: knees, back or shoulders complaining

The mistake

Pushing through a sore area because it is on the plan.

The fix

Swap the pattern, keep the session. Sore knees: skip sit-to-stands, keep wall push-ups and the hinge. Sore shoulders: skip wall push-ups, keep sit-to-stands and the march. Sore lower back: skip the hinge, keep heel raises and wall push-ups. In Sturdy you flag sore areas before a session and those movements are swapped out; mark one uncomfortable and it is left out in future.

Soreness that is sharp, swollen or getting worse is an injury question for a clinician, not a swap question.

Normal-ish day: actually feeling fine

The mistake

Turning a good day into a catch-up day and doing double.

The fix

Do the Standard Strength session as planned and stop at the end of it. The guideline minimum is muscle-strengthening work on at least two days a week; two honest sessions beat one heroic one followed by three days off. Log protein the easy way, checkmarks only if numbers are not your thing.

Questions from the tired days

Is it safe to work out when I feel tired on a GLP-1?

This page is general information, not medical advice, and it is written for adults using prescribed medication under medical care. Sturdy’s own listing says: if you have a medical condition, recent injury or fracture, dizziness or pain, check with your clinician before exercising. Tired is common (fatigue is listed at 11% vs 5% placebo for Wegovy); dizzy, faint or in pain is a different thing and a reason to stop.

Does exercise make the medication work better?

In the one-year Lundgren trial (NEJM, 2021), adults who paired a supervised exercise program with liraglutide after an 8-week diet lost 9.5 kg more than placebo at one year, versus 6.8 kg for the medication alone and 4.1 kg for exercise alone, and the combination group cut body fat by 3.9 percentage points and was the only group to improve cardiorespiratory fitness. It studied liraglutide, not semaglutide or tirzepatide, so treat it as a pointer, not a promise.

How much strength training do I need?

The Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, second edition, say adults need muscle-strengthening activity on at least two days each week, plus 150 to 300 minutes of moderate aerobic activity. The same edition removed the old ten-minute minimum: any amount of activity has some health benefit, which is the whole argument for a five-minute session on a bad day.

Should I exercise on injection day?

There is no single rule, and your prescriber’s guidance wins. A practical layout is gentle movement on dose day and the day after, with the harder sessions as far from the dose as the week allows, then adjust once you know your own side-effect pattern. Sturdy’s planner lets you move sessions freely and its optional medication records keep the dates you enter.

Sturdy lets you set your own target, switch to simple meal checkmarks, or turn the goal off. A clinician or dietitian who knows your weight, kidneys and medication can give you a number; this page does not.

No. The listing is explicit: Sturdy never suggests doses or changes schedules, and it never changes what you enter. Medication records are optional, and you can turn the whole section off.

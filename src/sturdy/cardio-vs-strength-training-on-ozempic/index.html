    How to record a mixed session

    If a 30-minute session included both walking and lifting, you can log it once as “Mixed.” If you know you walked for 20 minutes and lifted for 10, enter those separately instead. Both methods give a 30-minute total.

    The minutes describe what you recorded. Your care team can help with questions about which activities fit your circumstances.

Three ways people handle it

Force the plate

Three big meals like before. On a GLP-1 that is half a plate at each, nausea, and a quiet 40 g day that looks like effort.

Give up counting

Understandable. It usually means yoghurt for lunch and nothing until eight, for weeks, which is the pattern that costs muscle.

Divide and log lightly

Small moments, protein first, grams off the label when there is one and an honest estimate when there is not, consistency over perfection. That is the six steps below and how Sturdy's protein log is built.

Six steps, with the real screens

1

Set your own target, not the internet's

Research in people losing weight and training points to roughly 1.2 to 1.6 g per kg a day; dietitians quoted by TODAY put the floor at around 60 g for people on these medications. Pick a number with your clinician or dietitian. Sturdy lets you set it yourself, or turn the goal off and use meal checkmarks instead.

2

Split it into small meals

A 90 g day is impossible as three big plates on a GLP-1 and ordinary as four or five 20 g moments. The builder above does the division. Dietitians in the TODAY piece say the same: smaller, more frequent, spread across the day.

3

Protein first on the plate

When you can only manage half of what is in front of you, make the half you eat the protein. Dr Louis Aronne's advice via TODAY: vegetables and protein first, carbohydrates after.

4

Log from the label, or by hand

Grams straight off a packet when there is one. When there is not, Sturdy offers hand-portion estimates, a palm or a fist, that are clearly labelled as estimates and that you can correct. Guessing is fine; pretending a guess is a measurement is not.

5

Liquid days are allowed

On a nauseous day a shake or a glass of milk counts, and dietitians say low-calorie protein drinks are a reasonable fallback when whole foods will not go down. Ginger or peppermint tea, bland food, nothing greasy. If nausea persists, that is a conversation with your prescriber about dose, not a reason to stop eating.

6

Count consistency, not perfection

One heroic 120 g day followed by three yoghurt days is the pattern that loses muscle. Sturdy's progress view shows protein consistency, how many days you got near your own target, without scores or age norms. Near the target most days beats nailing it twice.

Sources: dietitian guidance in TODAY's piece on eating well on Ozempic or Wegovy; the 1.6 g/kg plateau from Morton and colleagues, British Journal of Sports Medicine, 2018; the basics of dietary protein at MedlinePlus.

Week six, a kitchen at 7 am

A composite scenario, not a customer story.

Tom is eight weeks into a prescribed GLP-1 and has stopped being hungry in the way other people mean the word. He eats because the clock says so, and most days the clock says so twice. His target, agreed with the practice dietitian, is 95 g. His honest average, when he finally logs a week, is 48.

What changes is not willpower. He sets the slider to 95 and the moments to five, and the number per moment comes out at 19. Nineteen grams is a pot of Greek yoghurt and a cheese stick. It is two eggs and a glass of milk. It is a scoop in water on the morning the smell of toast is too much. He logs the yoghurt from the label and the chicken by palm, marked as an estimate, and the week's consistency comes out at five days near target instead of one.

The scale moves about the same. The chair stand does not go backwards. That was the point.

Questions people ask

How much protein should I eat on Ozempic if I have no appetite?

Dietitians quoted by TODAY suggest at least around 60 g a day as a floor, spread across the day; research on people who are losing weight and lifting points to 1.2 to 1.6 g per kg. The right number for you depends on kidney health and what your clinician says. This is general information, not medical advice. Talk to your clinician.

Do protein shakes count?

Yes. A scoop in milk is around 25 to 28 g and goes down on days when food will not. Whole food is better when you can manage it, for the fibre and the rest, but a shake beats a skipped meal.

Why does it matter if I am losing weight anyway?

Because some of what you lose in a fast deficit is lean tissue, and protein plus resistance training are the two things with evidence for keeping most of it. Our page on lean mass vs muscle mass goes through the trial numbers.

What if I feel sick after eating?

Smaller portions, bland and low-fat, eaten slowly, without a big drink alongside, and not lying down straight after, per the dietitian advice above. If it keeps happening, the prescriber may want to look at the dose. Do not add anti-nausea medication on your own.

Does Sturdy tell me what to eat?

No. It is a strength planner and protein log for adults using GLP-1 or other prescribed weight-management medication under medical care. You set the target; it records what you log, by grams or by meal checkmarks, and shows consistency without judgement. It gives no dosing or injection advice.

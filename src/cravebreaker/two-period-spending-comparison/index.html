Why totals alone can mislead

A total of 40 over 7 days and 40 over 14 days are equal totals but different daily averages. This tool divides each total by the days you entered. It does not verify receipts or explain why spending changed.

Fictional entries, both in CAD: the earlier total is 84 over 28 days, or 3 per day. The later total is 56 over 14 days, or 4 per day. Although the later total is smaller, its daily average is 1 higher. Divide each amount by the days it covers before comparing daily averages. Use your own records in the worksheet above. A spending difference does not explain why spending changed, and the comparison does not measure a health or quitting outcome.

An original 20-second silent illustration by Honest Little Apps. This is a separate browser-worksheet example, not native-app footage or a customer record. Four original text-and-shape frames use hypothetical CAD 84/28 days and CAD 56/14 days. Arithmetic: 3/day, 4/day, later minus earlier +1/day. No health, abstinence, causal, traffic or savings claim.

Portable renderer: `tools/render-cravebreaker-period-video.py` (Pillow, FFmpeg and system DejaVu Sans). No stock, app UI, music or external media. Captions and an HTML transcript provide equivalent content. No autoplay. Stable canonical and existing Apple destination preserved.

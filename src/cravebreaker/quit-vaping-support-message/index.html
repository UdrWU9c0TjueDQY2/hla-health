How to ask someone for quit-vaping support

Choose someone you trust and name one action that would help. A request to avoid offering a vape is different from a request to check in every day. The draft makes those choices explicit instead of assuming every kind of encouragement suits you.

Before you share

Read the message in your own voice. Remove anything you do not want to disclose. Ask whether the person can offer that support; a prepared message does not guarantee agreement or availability.

Three ways to make the request more useful

  • Choose a specific action rather than asking someone to monitor you.
  • Say whether you want listening, practical company or a check-in.
  • Set a privacy preference, while recognizing that a request is not a confidentiality guarantee.

If the first person cannot help

You can ask another trusted person or seek professional support. For concerns about nicotine withdrawal or quitting, speak with a qualified health professional or a local quit service. This writing tool does not assess symptoms or provide treatment.

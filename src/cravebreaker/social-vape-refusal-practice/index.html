How to say no when someone offers you a vape

Short answer. Clear next move.

You do not owe a speech. Rehearse one sentence for the first offer, one boundary if they push, and one exit when the room is not helping.

×
“Just take one hit. It’s not a big deal.”
“No thanks. I quit.”
Decline → Boundary → Exit

Use only as much response as the situation needs. A short no is complete; the later steps exist if someone keeps pushing.

Delivery matters

Make the line easier to use.

The best rehearsal is small enough to remember under pressure. It does not need to explain your entire quit plan or convince the other person.

01

Keep the first answer short.

“No thanks, I quit” is a complete response. Add details only if you want to.

02

Name what you need.

If offers continue, “Please don’t offer me one” makes the boundary observable.

03

Pair words with movement.

Change seats, step outside, call someone or leave. The exit is an option, not a punishment.

Before a social event

Build a tiny plan, not a perfect night.

  • Choose one sentence you can say without thinking.
  • Tell one supportive person what would help.
  • Decide where you can step away for a few minutes.
  • Keep your own transport or exit option when practical.
  • Use a quitline or healthcare professional when you need more support.

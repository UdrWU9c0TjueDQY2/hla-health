Six mythsSources at the end
Myth

Everyone needs the same winter goal.

Fact

Daily fluid needs vary. The CDC describes differences with activity, age and other factors. A calculator with just a clock and a bottle log cannot set your target.

Myth

Only bottled water counts toward total fluid.

Fact

The CDC includes other beverages and moisture in food. This counter only reflects the amounts you enter.

Myth

Fluid ounces and ounces of weight are interchangeable.

Fact

This page measures volume. Choose US fluid ounces or millilitres to match the markings on your container.

Myth

A bottle size tells you how much you drank.

Fact

A 500 mL bottle that is still half full accounts for 250 mL consumed if it started full. Enter what you drank.

Myth

A pace mark tells you when to drink.

Fact

The mark is a straight-line arithmetic reference between the hours you enter. It cannot assess thirst or hydration.

Myth

A night shift needs a different calculator.

Fact

The time window can cross midnight. Enter the start and end of your waking period in local time.

QuestionsShort answers
How much water should you drink in winter?

Fluid needs vary. Enter a personal goal if you already have one, and follow any fluid guidance from your clinician. This tool does not determine a goal.

What does the pace mark mean?

It shows the fraction of your chosen hours that has elapsed, applied to your own goal. Being above or below that line is a mathematical comparison.

What happens outside my waking hours?

The counter still shows the amount left to your goal. It pauses the pace comparison until your selected window starts again.

Does the counter save my log?

No. Values stay in this browser tab and reset on reload. Nothing transfers to Dropkin.

What happens when I change units?

Both the goal and the logged amount convert between millilitres and US fluid ounces. One US fluid ounce is approximately 29.5735 millilitres.

What does Dropkin include for free?

The iPhone app includes water logging, saved vessels, reminders, Hydration Pace and 30-day history for free. Optional Pro adds more appearance choices and longer history.

    From intention to an ordinary moment

    Three details to decide once.

    01 / The place

    Choose one allowed, accessible spot. If you move between rooms, use your break area or another place you already return to.

    02 / The cue

    Pick a real event: opening your notebook, taking an existing break, or packing your bag. Edit the wording so it sounds like your day.

    03 / The record

    Log only what you actually drank. A reminder is not a completed drink; checking the bottle does not mean it is empty.

    Before you print.

    Will this send me reminders?

    No. This is a printable planning tool. It cannot send notifications or track drinks. For on-device reminders and logging, see Dropkin.

    How much water should I drink?

    This tool does not set an intake target. Its cues only remind you to check your bottle. Use your own appropriate guidance, including any clinician-directed fluid restrictions.

    Are my cue cards saved?

    No. The fields stay in this page while it is open, but this tool does not save them or send them anywhere. Copy or print before refreshing or leaving.

    Can I make just one card?

    Yes. Keep the cue that fits and delete the other two. At least one cue and a bottle location are needed to build your cards.

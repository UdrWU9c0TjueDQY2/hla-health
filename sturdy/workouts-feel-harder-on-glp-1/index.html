Why do workouts feel harder on a GLP-1, and what helps?

Short answer: you are training in a calorie deficit you did not plan, usually with less protein than before, and some days with nausea. Strength drops when sessions get skipped and protein stays low; the trials that measured body composition on semaglutide found a meaningful share of weight lost was lean mass. The fixes below are about making the unit of exercise small enough to survive a bad week, and making protein easy enough to hit when you are not hungry.

Mistake 1

You kept the old 45-minute plan

Appetite drops, calories drop, and the plan that fit your pre-medication week now ends in skipped sessions. Skipped sessions compound: a week off costs more strength than a short session ever would.

Fix: shrink the unit. Sturdy's Quick Movement session is about five minutes, Standard Strength runs up to 30 minutes on the free tier, and a missed day is left unfilled, not marked as a failure.

Mistake 2

Protein fell with your appetite

Weight loss on semaglutide took lean mass with it in the STEP 1 body-composition sub-study (roughly 40% of the weight lost). Resistance training plus enough protein is what protects muscle during a deficit, and "enough" is hard when you are not hungry.

Fix: a protein floor by meal period, logged the easy way. Sturdy logs grams from a label, hand-portion estimates (clearly marked as estimates), favourites and saved meals, or simple checkmarks if numbers stress you.

Mistake 3

Training through nausea or a sore site

Injection-week nausea and a sore hip are real; pushing through either usually buys a lost week. Soreness is a signal to swap, not to stop.

Fix: flag sore areas before a session and those movements are swapped out. Mark a movement uncomfortable and it is left out next time. Difficulty only changes when you choose.

Mistake 4

Judging the scale, not the strength

The scale cannot tell fat from muscle. A falling number with falling strength is the outcome you are trying to avoid, and you will not see it without a functional check.

Fix: the 30-second chair stand, based on the CDC STEADI protocol, plus balance and wall-sit checks. Sturdy keeps the history without scores, age norms or medical conclusions.

Mistake 5

No plan for the appointment

"How is the exercise going?" gets a shrug. Clinicians adjust medication, protein advice and referrals based on what you can show them.

Fix: a 30-day PDF summary of sessions, check-ins, protein and medication records, every section optional and all of it marked self-recorded. You choose when to share it.

What the fixes look like on the phone

A made-up week, eight weeks in

She lifted twice a week for years. Eight weeks into semaglutide the Thursday session kept getting moved to Saturday, then to never, because by Thursday evening she had eaten a yoghurt and half a sandwich. The fix was not motivation. It was a 20-minute Tuesday, a five-minute Thursday she could do in the kitchen, eggs at breakfast logged as "2 eggs" rather than weighed, and a chair-stand count every other Sunday so the question at the clinic had an answer: 11 in September, 13 in October.

Who should not use this page's plan

Anyone not under medical care for the medication

Sturdy is for adults using GLP-1 or other prescribed weight-management medication under medical care. If that is not you, a general strength app and your own clinician's advice fit better.

People with kidney disease or a protein restriction

The protein ranges here come from general weight-loss research. Protein targets with kidney disease, liver disease or during pregnancy are individual; use your clinician's number, not ours.

Severe or persistent symptoms

Vomiting, dehydration, dizziness on standing, or pain at an injection site that worsens are reasons to contact your prescriber, not to adjust a workout.

Questions people ask in week eight

Why do workouts feel harder on a GLP-1?

Mostly energy intake: GLP-1 medications reduce appetite, so you train with fewer calories and often less protein and fluid. Add nausea in the days after an injection and sessions planned for your old appetite stop fitting. Shorter, more frequent strength sessions and a protein floor are the common adjustments; your prescriber or a dietitian can tailor them.

How much protein should I eat while on semaglutide or tirzepatide?

Research on weight loss generally points to about 1.2 to 1.6 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight per day to help preserve lean mass, spread across meals. People with kidney disease or other conditions need an individual target, so treat the calculator on this page as a conversation starter for your clinician, not a prescription.

Is strength training safe during the nausea weeks?

Short, low-intensity movement is usually tolerated better than long sessions, and stopping when you feel light-headed matters. Sturdy's sessions can be paused, shortened or ended at any point, and the movement library lists when to stop for each exercise. If nausea, dizziness or vomiting is severe or persistent, contact your prescriber.

Does the app change my medication dose or schedule?

No. Sturdy records the medication, form, dose and dates you enter, with optional local reminders on the schedule you set. It never suggests doses or changes schedules, and the records can be switched off entirely.

A protein floor to discuss with your clinician

102 to 136 g

About 1.2 to 1.6 g of protein per kilogram of body weight per day, which research on weight loss associates with better lean-mass retention. Spread it across three or four meals; Sturdy logs by meal period.
